5. Messages, tickets, and moderation

The Bot processes Discord messages and related events to handle commands, support tickets, configured word/link filtering, message sniping, and staff activity counts. Ticket messages can be relayed between a member's DMs and a server ticket channel. When configured, closing a ticket generates a text transcript and sends it to the server's configured ticket-log channel. The ticket channel is then scheduled for deletion, but the transcript and other copies in Discord are not automatically deleted by that channel deletion.
