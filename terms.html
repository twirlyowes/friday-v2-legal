1. Accepting these terms

By inviting, configuring or using the bot or the dashboard, you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the service, and remove the bot from any server you manage.

2. What F.R.I.D.A.Y is

F.R.I.D.A.Y is an independently maintained Discord application. Depending on what a server enables, it provides server settings, moderation tools, AutoMod, Anti-Nuke and AntiBot protection, verification, ModMail support tickets, temporary voice rooms, staff activity reports, message publishing, quote images, utilities and optional YouTube notifications. The web dashboard lets authorised users view and change supported settings for servers they manage.

4. Server owners and administrators

If you invite or configure F.R.I.D.A.Y, you confirm that you have authority to do so and to grant the permissions it requests. Server owners and administrators are responsible for:

  • role hierarchy, channel permissions and the feature choices made for their server;
  • moderation decisions, including decisions influenced by automated rules;
  • keeping ticket, log and moderation channels restricted to appropriate staff; and
  • telling their members how the server uses the bot and what data it processes.

Delegated owners, which the server owner can add on the dashboard, have the same responsibilities for the areas they can manage.

5. Permissions the bot uses

The bot's permissions depend on the features you enable. Common permissions include Manage Roles, Manage Channels, Manage Messages, Kick Members, Ban Members, Moderate Members, View Audit Log and Manage Webhooks. Discord's own permission and role-hierarchy rules still apply.

FRIDAY Shield. When Anti-Nuke is turned on, from a command or the dashboard, FRIDAY creates a role named FRIDAY Shield with the Administrator permission. It places the role directly below the bot's highest role and gives the role to the bot. Administrator bypasses channel permission settings in the server. The server owner should understand that this gives the bot very broad access, and that anyone who controls the bot's account or token could use that access. Turning Anti-Nuke off with antinuke disable stops monitoring but keeps the role. The server owner can remove the role at any time, but removing it may stop Anti-Nuke from working.

6. Acceptable use

You must not use F.R.I.D.A.Y to break the law or violate other people's rights; to harass, threaten or impersonate people; to run raids, spam or scams; to disrupt Discord or FRIDAY; to bypass security or access controls, including the dashboard's Discord permission checks; or to collect or disclose personal information without proper authority.

Do not send passwords, payment details, government identification or other sensitive information through the bot, tickets, appeals or the dashboard. The service is not designed to request these.

7. Moderation and automated enforcement

Automated features act on rules that a server chooses to enable. They can delete messages, time members out, remove roles, quarantine members, kick or ban members, and remove or recreate channels and roles, depending on the settings. Automated decisions can be wrong. Server staff remain responsible for reviewing outcomes, correcting mistakes and keeping moderation logs.

Anti-Nuke responds to mass deletions, mass bans and kicks, webhook creation, unauthorised bots and dangerous permission changes. It depends on Discord's audit log, which can be delayed or incomplete. It cannot stop an attack that Discord's permissions do not allow the bot to stop, and it does not guarantee that every harmful change will be reversed.

Do not rely on F.R.I.D.A.Y as your only moderation, security, backup or incident-response system.

8. ModMail, tickets and appeals

Members can message the bot to open a support ticket with a server's staff. Messages may be relayed between the member's direct messages and a ticket channel in the server. When closing a ticket, the server's ticket-log channel may receive a transcript, and that copy is kept in Discord until someone deletes it. Deleting the ticket channel does not delete transcripts or other copies.

An appeal asks staff to review a punishment. Submitting an appeal does not remove or reverse the punishment. Staff decide each appeal, and their decision is final within the server.
