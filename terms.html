The bot may process messages and related events to handle commands, support tickets, configured filters, message sniping, quote images and staff activity. Server staff remain responsible for decisions made using warnings, strikes, timeouts, kicks, bans, verification or activity reports. An appeal submission requests review; it does not automatically remove or reverse a punishment. Do not rely on F.R.I.D.A.Y as your only moderation, security, backup or incident-response system.
